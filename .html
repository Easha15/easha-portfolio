About

I don't just design.
I build visual identities.

I'm EASHA. I design brands that hold together everywhere they show up — on a factory banner, a LinkedIn feed, a landing page or a product box. I use AI as a studio assistant, never as a replacement for taste.

  1. Chapter 1

    The sketchbook years

    Messy pages, wrong colours, a lot of bad art. That's where I learned that an honest idea beats a perfect render.

  2. Chapter 2

    First real brands

    Logos, posters and social posts for local businesses taught me to design for people who have to live with the result.

  3. Chapter 3

    Industrial identity

    Building-material and manufacturing brands like VV National, Intact UPVC and VV Green — making technical products look confident.

  4. Now

    Craft, sped up by AI

    Hand-made concepts, refined with AI workflows so clients get more exploration without losing the human hand.

How I work

Design
process

Five steps, the same every time — so you always know where the project stands.

1

Discover

A conversation about your business, your customers and what success looks like.

2

Research

Competitors, category codes and references — so the work stands apart for a reason.

3

Design

Sketches first, then two or three directions refined into one strong idea.

4

Prototype

Mockups on real formats — pages, packs, posts — so you can judge it in context.

5

Deliver

Organised final files, guidelines and support while the work goes live.
